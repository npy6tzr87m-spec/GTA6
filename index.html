Entering a cheat

  1. Finish the story first.Cheats stay locked until the final mission is done. A save that hasn't reached the end rejects every code.
  2. Unlock the cheat.Each one has its own challenge, shown on its card. Challenges you finished during the story count. Check Pause menu › Progress › Cheats.
  3. Save manually.You'll want a clean save to go back to (see below).
  4. Open the phone.In free roam, press D-pad ↑.
  5. Hold L1 + R1 and enter the code.On the phone's home screen, keep both bumpers held while you tap the arrows. Some guides word it as "hold L1, press R1", so if a code doesn't take, keep both held the whole time.
  6. Watch for the confirmation.Tap Entry mode on any card to step through a code one big arrow at a time.

Before you cheat

  • Trophies and achievements stop unlocking for the rest of that session.
  • The game won't save while a cheat is active. To go back to normal, quit without saving and load your last save.
  • Anything you do while a cheat is on is lost when you quit, so cheats can't help you finish challenges.
  • Not usable during missions or online.
  • Console only: there is no PC version yet, so no PC codes.

Start here first hours

  • Fix the camera settings. Set Motion Blur Strength to 0, Look and Aim Sensitivity to 9 or 10, and Movement Speed to Run.
  • Play the Prologue in one sitting. Saving is greyed out until it ends, so a crash sends you back to the start.
  • Save by hand. Pause › Game › Save Game. Autosave only runs after missions and there's no mission replay, so use a fresh slot at the start of each chapter.
  • Never carry big cash. Pocket money is lost if you die or get busted. Use the stash under Jason's bedroom floor, then BuckMe once the Buck Me mission opens banking.
  • Check the mailbox. Pay from Brian and Donnie lands in the mailbox outside Jason's house. Brian's lockup keypad code is 7700.
  • Keep your guns. Only guns bought at Ammu-Nation come back after a death. Store the ones you pick up in your car's trunk.
  • Live in Focus mode. Press L3 + R3 (LS + RS): enemies show red, usable items yellow, cash green. White doors are open, red doors are alarmed.
  • Holster before crowds. Press L1 (LB). Anyone who sees a drawn gun calls the police.
  • Scan before you steal. Use the WAiNK app on a car first to check for an alarm or tracker.
  • Train a little every day. Weights raise Health; running, cycling or the treadmill raise Stamina; calm activities like fishing or pool raise Focus. Strength and Cardio have daily caps and fade if you stop. Focus gains stay.
  • Watch your Criminal Profile. Killing civilians, police or animals drags it down through five tiers, and one of them, Irredeemable, is permanent. Save before a rampage.
  • Rob smart. Bring your partner, both masked, for bigger jobs since they handle the crowd. Kill the alarm in the back room and destroy the tapes.
  • Switch characters freely after the Breakfast Run mission in Chapter 2: hold D-pad → and press R1 (RB).

Sources: OutputLag, Endgame Tools, AlcastHQ, GamingBolt

Don't miss things that expire

Before Chapter 1 ends (Tourist Trap)

  • Do Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter early. Both disappear. For the rum, go around the liquor store's left wall to the backyard (about $1,300).
  • Buy the long gun at Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento during Gear Up. Skipping it can stall the next call.
  • Ultimate Edition owners: use the Enduro, Crest Kayak and Ganado Retro Build now. They're gone after Chapter 1.
  • Do Lucia's balcony meditation. It can't be done later.
  • Empty the floor stash, then make a manual save.

Later

  • An hourglass icon on the map means a time-limited job. Do it right away. Race Against Yago (Chapter 3) and This is a Free Call From… (Chapter 4) are the known ones.
  • Missing these doesn't block any trophy or 100% completion.

Sources: OutputLag, PowerPyx walkthrough

Lose the cops the heat system

Stars show how hard police are coming. The red icons under them are what police know about you. Each icon has its own fix, and clearing one doesn't clear the others.

Hollow starsA crime was reported, but police don't know it was you.
Solid whitePolice can see you right now.
Flashing whiteYou broke line of sight and they're searching.
RedYou lost them but you're still in the red search zone. Leave it.

The search zone later shrinks to a neighbourhood "heat zone". Police there know who they want, so an officer who spots you with an uncleared icon restarts the chase. At six stars the search covers the whole map.

Sources: All Things How, AlcastHQ

Collectibles & trophies

  • No hidden packages this time. The closest thing is the 125 cocaine flamingos (100 count for 100%). Real ones have a black-and-white beak; decorative flamingos with yellow-and-black beaks don't count. Shoot the body and a package drops.
  • Cards: 480 in 18 sets, and 15 complete sets count for 100%. The Vice City Flamingos set (24 cards) unlocks the Bubblegum filter cheat.
  • Watches: 34 luxury watches sit inside the 79 discoverable styles, often at Gang Operations. Ten and twenty unlock the two time cheats.
  • Trophies: 55 in total (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze), all single-player. The platinum is Vice Got a Price! About half are hidden story trophies.
  • Track everything under Map › Progress, the in-game database of collectibles, vehicles, animals and weapons.

Sources: PowerPyx, ViceCheats, Push Square, ShowGamer, GamerFuzion
